🌊 PLASTIC NEVER DIES

✨ “That plastic bag you threw away? It came back.” ✨

🗺️ You won't believe where your plastic bottle ends up

You buy a drink, finish it in five minutes, toss it. But that bottle doesn't vanish. It travels to a landfill or gets swept into a drain → river → ocean. Waves and sun tear it into microplastics – invisible killers. Fish eat them. You eat the fish. Your own bottle returns inside you. Scientists now find microplastics in human blood, lungs, and unborn babies. Plastic never leaves; it just migrates home — to your dinner plate.

🧪 “Every piece of plastic ever created still exists on our planet – except the small fraction we've burned.”

🧴 There's something in your plastic bottle. And it's not safe.

The label says “pure spring water” — but heat makes plastic leach. A bottle left in a warm car releases microplastics + hormone-disrupting chemicals. You can't see them, but each sip adds another dose. Studies show that people who drink mostly bottled water swallow tens of thousands of microplastic particles every year. Is that “pure” refreshment worth the hidden poison?

♻️ That plastic bag you threw away? It came back (stronger).

It clogs drains, floats to the sea, breaks into fragments. Those fragments ride the current into seafood, table salt, tap water, and even the air you breathe. Microplastics have been found in Arctic snow and rain. Your daily life: plastic in your tea (from tea bags), in your clothes (polyester shedding), in the dust at home. No escape — but we CAN fight back.
